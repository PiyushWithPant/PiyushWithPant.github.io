## The Journey of Light

The light from the Sun takes **8 minutes and 20 seconds** to reach Earth. So if you are outside and you are feeling the warmth of the sunlight, that exact light particle (or photon) started its journey around 8 mins ago, and finally reached you!
Let's do some basic math to understand this. If you remember the classic speed, distance and time formula from the school 

$$
Distance = Speed \times Time
$$

Let me fill in the data you need to calculate the time for the light from the Sun to reach Earth. The Sun is around **150 million kilometers** away from us and the speed of light (the fastest thing in the entire universe!) is roughly **300,000 kilometers per SECOND**!

$$
Time = \frac{150,000,000 \; km}  {300,000 \; km/s} \implies 500 \; seconds \;
$$

That's about 8 minutes 20 second for ya!
If you think this is a surprising, hold on... let me remind you that the sun is the *closest* star to us. The second closest star is **Proxima Centauri** which is **4.24 light years** away from us or roughly **40 trillion kilometers** away. A light year is basically the distance travelled by light (the absolute fastest thing in the universe) in a year, which is around **9.46 trillion kilometers**, or thanks to google exactly **9,460,730,472,580 kilometers**.
The light from this star takes **4 years and 3 months** to reach us so when astronomers are looking through their telescope, they are seeing the light which left the star 4 years ago and reached us now.

### Expansion of the Space 

As you read these lines, space, THE space, is expanding. I mean literally increasing in the size every second. Now I think (and hope), you will be curious and ask why isn't my computer going far away from me then? The answer is - Gravity. 

Gravity is the local gangster of the neighbourhood. Scientifically, it is much stronger locally and dominates the effects of the expansion, and that is why all your belongings are exactly where they are and why earth is exactly the same distance away from the sun as it was 100 years ago. The problem comes when things are too far away from each other in the (maybe infinite) space, this makes the local gangster loose the war and the expansion starts to dominate. but how does this expansion affects us? doesn't seem that scary? and it might not be, but the problem is - It is not alone!


### (Accelerated) Expansion of the Space 

The rate of cosmic expansion is not constant. The universe is not expanding at the same speed today as it was one year or one million years ago; instead, the expansion rate is dynamically changing. Crucially, the velocity of this expansion is actively accelerating, driven by an elusive force known as dark energy.
