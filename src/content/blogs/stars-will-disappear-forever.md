## What are Stars actually?

We look at the sky at night and see all the *countless* twinkling stars but most of us do not know that they are actually **Suns** of another solar systems which far far away from us. The stars that is nearest to us is what we call the **Sun**. These stars are basically giant spheres of gas and plasma held together by the insanely strong gravity (strength of the gravity depends on Mass and Distance!). We can fit around **1,300,000** Earths inside the Sun so you can guess how gigantic the Sun is and that explains its insanely strong gravity, that not just pulls its own core together but also the entire solar system exists because of our dear Sun. ## The Journey of Light

The light from the Sun takes **8 minutes and 20 seconds** to reach Earth. So if you are outside and you are feeling the warmth of the sunlight, that exact light particle (or photon) started its journey around 8 mins ago, and finally reached you!
Let's do some basic math to understand this. If you remember the classic speed, distance and time formula from the school 

$$
Distance = Speed \times Time
$$

Let me fill in the data you need to calculate the time for the light from the Sun to reach Earth. The Sun is around **150 million kilometers** away from us and the speed of light (the fastest thing in the entire universe!) is roughly **300,000 kilometers per SECOND**!

$$
Time = \frac{150,000,000 \; km}  {300,000 \; km/s} \implies 500 \; seconds \;
$$

That's about 8 minutes 20 second for ya!
If you think this is a surprising, hold on... let me remind you that the sun is the *closest* star to us. The second closest star is **Proxima Centauri** which is **4.24 light years** away from us or roughly **40 trillion kilometers** away. A light year is basically the distance travelled by light (the absolute fastest thing in the universe) in a year, which is around **9.46 trillion kilometers**, or thanks to google exactly **9,460,730,472,580 kilometers**.
